# Balatro
> [!WARNING]
> **Work in progress**
## Currently working features:
### Hand system
- The amount of Cards you select display the according Hand on the UI
- Played Cards jump out
### Info
- Remaining Hands/Discards and current Money are displayed and updated on the UI
- The Played Cards' Chips are added to the Chip counter and the Chip * Multiplier score is updated
### Stake and Deck selection
- The Deck and Stake you select apply their Effect on your current Run
- You can unlock Difficulties per Deck and they are displayed accordingly
### Jokers
- Passive Jokers apply their Effect when they are bought
- Active Jokers apply their Effect when Cards are being played
- Bought Jokers are displayed on the UI
### Shop and packs
- Shop Rerolling
- Purchasing of Consumables, Jokers, Packs
### Achievements
- Tracking is implemented
- Unlocks tied to achievements is implemented
## Partially done:

- Packs and their application to Playing Cards
### Graphical elements

- Chips and Mult display
